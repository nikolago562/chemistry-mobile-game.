Origin / AI disclosure
----------------------
Method: AI-assisted (contains AI-generated content)
The sprites were rendered in Blender from rigged Grab3D 3D models. The base meshes and textures of these models
were generated with an AI image-to-3D tool (Meshy), then cleaned up, scaled, textured and checked in Blender.
Skeleton, skinning and the walk and run clips were added automatically with Meshy Auto-Rig.
The rendering scripts were written with the help of an AI coding assistant.
If your platform requires disclosure of AI-generated content (for example Steam), these sprites count as
AI-assisted.
